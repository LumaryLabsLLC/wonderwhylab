How it teaches

Learn by doing, then make it stick

Every lesson follows the same calm rhythm, so children always know what comes next.

  1. 1

    Play

    A hands-on playground to explore first: drag, sort, build and test, with labels that explain each part.

  2. 2

    How it works

    A short guide and a worked example, read aloud, for when your child wants to know why.

  3. 3

    Practice

    Five questions that get harder after three right in a row and easier after a slip. A hint after one try, a worked solution after two.

  4. 4

    Review

    Finished lessons come back a day, three days, a week, two weeks and a month later, mixed together, so they last.

FAQ

Questions grown-ups ask

What ages is it for?

Children aged 4 to 12, in four age groups: 4–5, 6–7, 8–9 and 10–12. You choose one when you start, and you can change it any time in Grown-ups. Games adapt to the age group too.

Does my child need to read?

No. Pip reads every prompt, question and story aloud, and answers are big buttons to tap or things to drag. Younger children can play on their own; older ones can turn reading aloud down in Grown-ups.

How much does it cost?

It’s free for 2 weeks, then $9.99 a month in the US (the App Store shows your local price). That one subscription unlocks everything: every lesson, game and story for every age group. There are no ads and nothing else to buy.

Which iPads does it work on?

Any iPad that runs iPadOS 26 or later. Wonder Why Lab is made for iPad, and works in landscape, portrait and resizable windows.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Lessons, games, stories and Pip’s voice are all on the iPad, so it works on planes, in the car and anywhere without Wi‑Fi. You only need a connection to download it and to start your subscription.

How do I cancel?

In the Settings app on your iPad, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Or in Wonder Why Lab, press and hold the gear on the home screen for two seconds to open Grown-ups, then tap Manage subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the free trial or the current month to avoid the next charge. Cancel during the free trial and you won’t be charged.

Can the whole family use it?

Wonder Why Lab keeps one set of progress on each iPad, with one name and age group. Brothers and sisters can take turns on the same iPad and switch the age group in Grown-ups, though they’ll share stars and stickers.

Your subscription works on every iPad signed in to the same Apple Account: open Grown-ups and tap Restore purchases.

What do you know about my child?

Nothing. There are no accounts, and the app has no ads, analytics or tracking. Your child’s optional first name and all their progress stay on your iPad. Read the privacy policy for the details.
