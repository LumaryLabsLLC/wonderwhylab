Here’s how we look after your family’s privacy.

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026 · Lumary Labs LLC

The short version

  • Wonder Why Lab has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. The app has no analytics, telemetry or crash uploads.
  • The app makes no network requests of its own. Your child’s name, age group and progress stay on your iPad.
  • Pip’s voice is recorded into the app or made on the iPad. Nothing your child says, types or does is sent to us.
  • We don’t collect personal information from children. We don’t sell data, and we don’t use it for advertising.

Lumary Labs LLC (“we”) makes Wonder Why Lab and is responsible for this policy. It covers the Wonder Why Lab app for iPad and this website, wonderwhylab.com. Write to us at contact@lumarylabs.com.

Children’s privacy

Wonder Why Lab is made for children aged 4 to 12, so we built it to work without collecting personal information from them at all.

  • There’s no sign-up, no account and no profile on our servers.
  • The first name you can give when you set up the app is optional. It’s used only on the iPad, so Pip can greet your child, and it never leaves the iPad.
  • There are no ads, no advertising or analytics tools from other companies, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • There’s no chat, no sharing and no way to contact other people. The app doesn’t ask for the camera, microphone, location, photos or contacts.
  • Buying the subscription is behind a grown-up check: you type, in digits, a number written out in words. The only links out of the app, to this policy and our terms, are on the subscription screen after that check and in the Grown-ups settings, which open only after pressing and holding a button for two seconds.

Because we don’t collect personal information from children, we have none to review or delete, and you stay in control of everything on the iPad (see below). If you’re a parent or guardian and think a child’s information has reached us anyway, for example in an email, write to us and we’ll delete it.

What stays on your iPad

The app keeps these in its own storage on the iPad. We don’t receive any of them.

  • Your child’s first name, if you gave one, and the age group you chose.
  • Progress: lessons and practice results, when each lesson is due for review, stars, stickers and game scores.
  • Grown-ups settings, such as sound, music, voice speed, relaxed games and brain breaks.

To remove them, open Grown-ups and choose Reset all progress, which clears the name, age group and all progress on that iPad. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. Like any app’s data, it may be included in backups of your iPad that you make to iCloud or a computer; those are between you and Apple.

Pip’s voice

Pip’s lines are recordings that come with the app, and longer lines in lessons and stories are spoken by a voice model that runs on the iPad itself. No text or audio goes to a speech service, and the app never listens through the microphone.

What the app connects to

Only Apple’s App Store, through StoreKit on your iPad, to start, check and restore the subscription. Apple’s privacy policy covers what it receives. The app makes no other network requests: no analytics, telemetry, crash uploads or ads.

  • Apple’s own reports. If you’ve turned on Share With App Developers in your iPad’s Settings (Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share crash reports and app usage statistics with us. Apple decides what’s in them, they don’t identify you, and you can turn this off at any time.
  • Links out. Opening the Terms of Use or Privacy Policy from Grown-ups takes you to this website in your browser, where “This website” below applies.

The subscription

Apple handles the subscription and your payment. The app asks the App Store on your iPad whether you’re subscribed, and sends nothing about your purchase to us. Apple gives us sales reports, not your name or payment details.

This website

  • No analytics or cookies. wonderwhylab.com has no analytics, sets no cookies and loads nothing from other websites. Its fonts and images come from the site itself.
  • Our host. Vercel hosts the site. As with any website, it handles your IP address and what your browser asks for, and may keep that in its logs for a limited time under its own policy. We don’t use these logs to identify you.
  • Email. If you write to us, we keep your email so we can answer it.

Who else handles data

Only the companies named here: Apple for the App Store, purchases and the optional reports above, and Vercel for this website. Some of them work outside your country, including in the United States. We’d also share data if the law required us to.

Your rights

Because Wonder Why Lab has no accounts and we keep so little, there’s usually nothing we can connect to you, apart from any email you sent us. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct or delete data about you, to object to how it’s used, and to complain to your data protection authority. Write to us and we’ll help.

Where the law asks for a reason to handle data, ours is that it’s needed to do what you asked for, such as answering your email, or to run and protect the website.

Changes

If this changes, we’ll post the new version here and change the date above. We won’t start collecting personal information from children without first updating this policy and, where the law requires it, getting a parent’s consent.
